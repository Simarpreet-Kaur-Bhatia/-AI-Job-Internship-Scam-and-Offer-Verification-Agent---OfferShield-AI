How OfferShield AI Works

A transparent, step-by-step verification workflow that shows you the evidence and reasoning behind every risk assessment.

🎯 The Problem We Solve

Students and job seekers receive offers via email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Telegram, and job portals. Some contain warning indicators most people don't know to look for:

  • Upfront registration or security fees
  • Personal email used for official recruiting
  • Urgency and pressure tactics
  • Company and recruiter information mismatches
  • Requests for sensitive financial information
  • Suspicious or shortened links
  • Non-standard recruitment processes
  • Vague or unverifiable company details
📋 5-Step Verification Workflow
1

Submit Your Offer

Upload an offer letter or document (PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG), paste a complete recruitment message, or enter an offer URL. No account required.

2

Extract Important Information

The Document Analysis Agent identifies company name, recruiter details, job title, salary, joining conditions, payment requests, URLs, and key clauses.

3

Analyse Verification Signals

Multiple specialised agents check for payment patterns, email domain consistency, urgency language, information mismatches, sensitive info requests, and non-standard recruitment processes.

4

Generate Explainable Risk Score

A transparent rule-based scoring system calculates a Risk Assessment Score (0–100). Every point is attributed to a specific indicator with cited evidence — nothing is hidden.

5

Take Informed Action

Receive specific, prioritised recommendations based on detected indicators. Know exactly what to verify, what to avoid, and what to confirm independently.
